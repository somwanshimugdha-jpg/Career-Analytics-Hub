Website Analytics

This page explains what can be measured after connecting this website to Google Analytics 4.

👥

Users

Understand how many users visit your website and how they interact with it.

👁️

Views

Measure page views and identify your most popular pages.

🖱️

Events

Track button clicks, form submissions, searches and other actions.

Suggested GA4 Events

  • Explore Careers button click
  • View Courses button click
  • Contact form submission
  • Blog page visits
  • External link clicks

How to connect Google Analytics

  1. Create a GA4 property in Google Analytics.
  2. Create a Web data stream for your website.
  3. Copy the Google tag provided by GA4.
  4. Paste it inside the <head> of every page.
  5. Publish the website and verify activity in Realtime.
